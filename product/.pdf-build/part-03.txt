## Commercial Product Structure

SnapBurst is intended to reach the unmanned-aircraft market through two related but distinct commercial routes.

Both routes use the same underlying governed-autonomy capability, but they serve different customers and deployment models.

### SnapBurst Standalone Product

SnapBurst is the standalone governed-autonomy product.

It is intended for unmanned-aircraft operators, programme owners and system integrators that need to add governed command execution, C2-loss handling, bounded mission continuation, controlled restoration and evidence to an existing aircraft operation.

The standalone product can be integrated around an existing combination of:

* aircraft platform;
* flight controller;
* mission system;
* autonomy system;
* communications system;
* fleet-management system;
* and operational-control environment.

The customer continues using its existing aircraft and autonomy technology.

SnapBurst adds the governance boundary between proposed actions and aircraft-command execution.

A standalone SnapBurst deployment may support:

* operator-specific integration;
* system-integration projects;
* pilot programmes;
* regulated operational trials;
* supervised BVLOS development;
* aircraft-fleet integration;
* assurance evidence generation;
* and operational governance around existing autonomous systems.

The standalone product remains identifiable as SnapBurst within the customer’s operating architecture.

### SnapBurstOS Integration and Licensing

SnapBurstOS is the third-party drone-industry integration and licensing form of the SnapBurst capability.

It is intended for:

* aircraft manufacturers;
* drone OEMs;
* autopilot and flight-stack providers;
* autonomy-system developers;
* fleet-platform companies;
* mission-platform providers;
* and other unmanned-aircraft technology companies.

SnapBurstOS allows those companies to incorporate SnapBurst governance into their own aircraft, autonomy or fleet products.

This route is not simply a standalone SnapBurst installation operated alongside the customer’s product.

It is the licensed product form for deeper third-party integration, embedding or commercial incorporation within the partner’s own unmanned-aircraft offering.

Depending on the agreed integration, SnapBurstOS may be incorporated at:

* the aircraft-command boundary;
* the autonomy-to-flight-controller interface;
* the fleet-command layer;
* the mission-execution layer;
* the aircraft supervisory layer;
* or another defined governance point within the partner architecture.

A SnapBurstOS licence may support:

* OEM product integration;
* embedded governed-autonomy capability;
* licensed software components;
* product-specific adaptation;
* aircraft-family integration;
* fleet-platform integration;
* partner-controlled deployment;
* and commercial distribution as part of a third-party drone product.

The third party retains responsibility for its aircraft, autonomy, mission logic, communications, operational approvals and product-specific safety case.

SnapBurstOS provides the licensed governance capability within that wider product.

SnapBurstOS remains specific to unmanned-aircraft applications.

A SnapBurstOS licence does not provide:

* unrestricted access to SnapSpace OS;
* ownership of the underlying deterministic governance core;
* cross-industry licensing rights;
* rights to apply SnapSpace OS outside the agreed unmanned-aircraft scope;
* or authority to redistribute the underlying core independently of the licensed SnapBurstOS product.

### SnapSpace OS Relationship

SnapSpace OS is the proprietary deterministic governance core beneath SnapBurst and SnapBurstOS.

It provides the underlying decision model and governance mechanisms from which the aircraft-specific products are constructed.

The commercial relationship is therefore:

**SnapSpace OS**
Proprietary cross-domain governance core.

**SnapBurst**
Standalone governed-autonomy product for unmanned-aircraft operators and system integrators.

**SnapBurstOS**
Licensed third-party drone-industry integration form for manufacturers, autonomy developers and fleet-platform providers.

This structure allows SnapBurst capability to be sold directly as a standalone product while also supporting deeper licensed incorporation into third-party unmanned-aircraft products without exposing or transferring unrestricted rights to the underlying SnapSpace OS technology.

## Product Value

SnapBurst addresses the operational trust gap between autonomous decisions and physical aircraft actions.

Its value is not simply that an aircraft can continue operating when communications or command conditions change.

Its value is that continued operation remains:

* authorised;
* bounded;
* attributable;
* isolated by aircraft;
* recoverable;
* reviewable;
* and auditable.

SnapBurst provides a controlled path from supervised aircraft operation toward governed autonomous operation without replacing the aircraft, flight controller, communications system, mission-autonomy platform or detect-and-avoid system.
